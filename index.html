Research

My work spans photovoltaics, photocatalysis, electrocatalysis, spintronics and metal-ion batteries, with a focus on low-dimensional and nanostructured materials. It has led to 27 peer-reviewed articles, with one more in press.

Excited carriers and photocatalysis

Non-adiabatic molecular dynamics of hot-carrier relaxation, charge transfer and recombination in van der Waals heterostructures, COFs and halide perovskites, and what they mean for solar water splitting and nitrogen fixation.

Machine-learned atomistic simulation

Graph neural networks that make molecular dynamics fast enough to sample reactive interfaces, including E(q)C-GNN and GNN-accelerated studies of photocatalytic nitrogen reduction.

Electrocatalysis and interfaces

Adsorption energetics, defects and surface terminations at electrochemical interfaces, developed alongside experimental partners to guide the design of better electrocatalysts.

Energy storage

First-principles design of 2D electrode materials for Li- and Na-ion batteries and bifunctional catalysts for Li–air cells.
