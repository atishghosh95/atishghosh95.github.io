# Hi, I'm Atish Ghosh 👋

Computational chemist and postdoctoral research fellow at the **University of Massachusetts Amherst**.
I use density functional theory, ab initio and non-adiabatic molecular dynamics, and graph neural networks
to understand how charge, light and ions move through materials for clean energy.

🌐 **Website:** [atishghosh95.github.io](https://atishghosh95.github.io)
🔬 **Research explained simply:** [atishghosh95.github.io/explained.html](https://atishghosh95.github.io/explained.html)

### Research interests
- Non-adiabatic molecular dynamics (NAMD) and hot-carrier dynamics
- Machine-learned molecular dynamics with graph neural networks (E(q)C-GNN)
- Photocatalytic water splitting and nitrogen reduction on 2D materials and COFs
- Electrocatalysis and electrochemical interfaces
- 2D electrode materials for Li/Na-ion and Li–air batteries

### Selected work
- **ACS Catal.** 16, 3284–3295 (2026): GNN-assisted photocatalytic nitrogen reduction on GaS
- **J. Phys. Chem. Lett.** 16, 2254–2264 (2025): E(q)C-GNN for accelerating molecular dynamics
- **J. Phys. Chem. Lett.** 15, 4898–4905 (2024): hot-carrier-controlled nitrogen fixation on a boron-doped aza-COF
